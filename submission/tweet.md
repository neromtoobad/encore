## Post (long post: needs X Premium, over 280 characters)

Every tour plays the same big cities. So does everyone else's. That's why the first question an artist asks, "where are my fans?", is the wrong one. The useful question is: which cities love work like mine, and aren't getting enough of it?

So I built Encore for the @Qloo Agentic Hackathon. It's for anyone who takes their work on the road: independent musicians and their managers, comedians, podcasters doing live shows, authors on book tours, brands doing pop-ups, film screenings, game studios and chefs running supper clubs.

You type in what you take on the road. Encore scans 42 cities across North America, the UK, Europe and Africa. Qloo's taste graph shows how strongly your audience shows up in each city. Ticketmaster shows what's already booked there in the next 90 days, including dates by the acts your fans love most, because those are your real competition for the same people's nights out. High demand with little booked is whitespace.

Example: Asake. London is in his top 15% of places, but Omah Lay and Adekunle Gold both play there in November, so it's crowded. Washington and Atlanta rank about as high with none of those acts booked. Open any city and you get the evidence: where the crowd lives, venues they already like, support acts with the same fans, podcasts for promo.

Then a Claude agent plans the run. Give it a brief like "7 theatre dates, skip anywhere crowded" and it inspects each city, routes the dates around clashing shows, and writes the outreach to venues, openers and promo partners. When it only found 6 cities that weren't crowded, it left the 7th date to me instead of making one up.
