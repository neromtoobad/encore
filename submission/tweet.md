## Main post

Every tour plays the same big cities. So does everyone else's.

I built Encore for @qloo's Agentic Hackathon. It finds the cities that love your work but aren't getting enough of it, then a Claude agent plans the run and writes the outreach.

Try it: https://encore.nerom.site

## Thread (optional replies)

2/
How it works: Qloo's taste graph shows where your audience over-indexes across 42 cities. Ticketmaster shows what's already booked there, including dates by the acts your fans love most.

High demand, little booked: that's whitespace.

3/
Example: Asake. London ranks in his top 15%, but Omah Lay and Adekunle Gold both play there in November, so it's crowded.

Washington and Atlanta rank about as high, with none of those acts booked. That's where to go.

4/
The agent takes a brief like "7 theatre dates, skip anywhere crowded", inspects each city, routes around clashing shows and drafts the venue, opener and promo emails.

It found only 6 uncrowded markets, so it left the 7th to me instead of faking one.

5/
Same engine for musicians, comedians, podcasts, authors, brand pop-ups, films, games and chefs.
